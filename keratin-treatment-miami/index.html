Keratin & treatments

Frizz control for Miami weather

A keratin treatment smooths the hair cuticle so your hair resists humidity, cuts down blow-dry time and stays manageable for weeks.

Not sure if you need keratin or a lighter treatment? Your stylist looks at your hair and recommends the right one at your consultation.

  • Keratin: long-lasting smoothing and frizz control
  • Hair Botox: deep conditioning that restores softness and shine
  • Olaplex, Lanza Trauma and ampoules: repair and strengthen

Good to know

How much is a keratin treatment?

Women's keratin starts at $349 and men's at $100. Length and thickness set the final price.

What's the difference between keratin and hair Botox?

Keratin is a smoothing treatment for frizz. Hair Botox is a deep conditioning treatment that restores softness and shine. Your stylist will tell you which suits your hair.
