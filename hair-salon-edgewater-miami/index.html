About the salon

A full-service salon on the bay

YSV has been part of Edgewater since 2014, inside Quantum on the Bay on N Bayshore Drive. It's a boutique salon, so you get a personal welcome and a stylist who remembers your hair.

Hair and spa share one roof: book a balayage and a facial, or a cut and a manicure, in the same visit.

  • Free valet parking on every visit
  • Bilingual team: English and Spanish
  • A dedicated men's grooming side
  • Open Monday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.

What we do

Hair services in Edgewater

Balayage and highlights from $149, tape-in and keratin extensions, keratin smoothing and hair Botox for Miami humidity, plus cuts, blowouts and updos for women, men and kids.

Good to know

Where is YSV in Edgewater?

Inside Quantum on the Bay at 1900 N Bayshore Dr, Suite 206, Miami, FL 33132, near the Venetian Causeway.
