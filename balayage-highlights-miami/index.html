Professional hair coloring

A custom formula, every time

When it comes to color, our team never works from a stock shade. Your colorist looks at your natural base, your skin tone and how often you want to come back, then mixes a formula for you.

We offer highlights, ombré, balayage, color correction and repair treatments for retouched or damaged hair.

Which technique is right for you?

Balayage is painted by hand for a soft, grown-out look with gentle regrowth.

Foil highlights give more lift and a brighter, more even result.

Gloss refreshes tone and shine between color appointments.
