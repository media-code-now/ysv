How it works

What is hair Botox?

Despite the name, there's no botulinum toxin and no injections. Hair Botox is a conditioning treatment that coats and fills the hair fiber with a blend of proteins, oils and nutrients.

Damaged strands get smoother and stronger, so your hair looks fuller and shinier and is easier to style. Your stylist applies the treatment, lets it work, then seals it in with heat.

  • Softens dry, brittle and over-processed hair
  • Adds shine and body
  • Tames frizz without flattening your natural texture
  • A good fit for bleached and color-treated hair

Choosing a treatment

Hair Botox or keratin?

Keratin is a smoothing treatment. It's the one to choose if your main goal is straighter, frizz-free hair that holds up in Miami humidity.

Hair Botox is about repair. It brings back softness, shine and strength while keeping more of your natural movement and volume.

Not sure? Your stylist looks at your hair at the consultation and recommends one, or a mix of treatments.

Questions

Good to know

Is hair Botox actual Botox?

No. There's no botulinum toxin and nothing is injected. The name refers to the smoothing, filling effect on damaged hair.

How much is hair Botox at YSV?

Hair Botox starts at $349. Longer or thicker hair takes more product and time, so your stylist confirms the price at your consultation.

Can I get hair Botox on color-treated or bleached hair?

Yes. It's a good choice for hair that's dry or damaged from color and lightening. Tell your stylist about any recent chemical services.

How should I care for my hair after?

Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner to make the results last longer. Your stylist will give you aftercare tips for your hair.
