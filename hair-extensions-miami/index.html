Extensions at YSV

Two methods, one seamless result

Whatever you want, there is a way to get it. For extra length or volume you can choose Hairtalk tape-in extensions or keratin extensions.

Your stylist matches the color and texture of your own hair, then cuts and blends the extensions in so the result looks like it grew that way.

  • Tape-in: quick to apply and easy to move up as your hair grows
  • Keratin: individual bonds for a very natural fall and movement
  • Color matched and blended by your stylist
