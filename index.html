Founder & creative director

Yair Vaknin

Yair founded YSV in 2011 with a salon on Lincoln Road in South Beach, then opened the Edgewater salon on the bay in 2014. It pairs his master stylists with a full spa team and a dedicated men's barbering side.

The idea is simple: a salon that feels personal, where you can park, unwind, and leave looking and feeling your best.

Questions

Before your first visit

Where is YSV Hair Salon & Spa?

Inside Quantum on the Bay at 1900 N Bayshore Dr, Suite 206, Miami, FL 33132, in the Edgewater neighborhood.

Is parking free?

Yes. Valet parking is complimentary for every appointment.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Our team is bilingual in English and Spanish. ¡Hablamos español!

Do you offer hair extensions and keratin treatments?

Yes. We offer hair extensions, keratin smoothing, balayage and highlights, plus spa services including HydraGlow facials, brow shaping, manicures and pedicures.

How much does balayage cost at YSV?

Balayage starts at $249, full highlights at $249 and partial highlights at $149. Women's haircuts start at $85 and keratin at $349. Your stylist confirms the final price at a consultation.

Do you cut men's hair?

Yes. Men's haircuts start at $50, beard trims start at $20 and kids' cuts are $40. We also offer men's color, highlights and keratin.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.
