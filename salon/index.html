Our story

From Lincoln Road to Edgewater

Yair Vaknin founded YSV in 2011 to open a full-service hair salon. Because of his love for South Beach, he chose Lincoln Road, where he could leave his mark on locals and feel the energy of visitors from around the world.

Ambition and passion pushed him further, and in 2014 he opened the salon in Edgewater, on the bay.

The team

High-end, but personal

The look and feel set the tone for a high-end beauty experience, and our highly trained team keeps it friendly and personal.

Color, extensions, keratin and smoothing, custom cuts and styling, a dedicated men's barbering side, and a full spa.
