Scalp micropigmentation in Miami

Thinning hair and hair loss aren't something you just have to accept. SMP restores the look of a fuller head of hair or a sharp, close-shaved hairline.

How it works

What is scalp micropigmentation?

SMP uses microfine pigment, applied with devices designed for the scalp, to replicate the look of natural hair follicles. Layered shades and patterns create depth and definition, from a buzz-cut look to a crisp edge-up.

It helps men who want to shave their head but hold back because of scalp spots, and women who are starting to notice thinning hair.

  • Hair thickening
  • Micropigmentation for baldness
  • Scar cover-up
  • Camouflage for scalp spots, burns and birthmarks
  • Alopecia correction

How long it lasts

4 to 8 years before a touch-up

The pigment is permanent, but Florida sun and the skin's natural exfoliation fade it over time. SMP usually keeps its original look for 4–8 years, then a quick touch-up restores it. Skin tone and level of hair loss also play a part.

Why people choose it

An alternative to a transplant

  • Works for people who aren't transplant candidates
  • Helps thinning hair as well as hair loss
  • No surgery and no recovery time

SMP doesn't grow hair. It creates the appearance of thicker hair or a close-cropped style.

Questions

Good to know

Does SMP grow hair?

No. SMP doesn't stimulate hair growth or transplant follicles. It uses pigment to create the look of thicker hair or a shaved style.
