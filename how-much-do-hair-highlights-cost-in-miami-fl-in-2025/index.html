Partial, full or balayage?

Partial highlights brighten the top and around the face, and are the most budget-friendly way to add dimension.

Full highlights run through the whole head for an all-over brighter result, so they take longer and use more product.

Balayage is painted by hand for a soft, natural grow-out. It often costs about the same as full highlights up front but needs fewer touch-ups.

What changes the price

  • Length and thickness: more hair takes more product and more time.
  • How light you want to go: big changes may need extra lightening or more than one session.
  • Color correction: fixing previous color is quoted separately at consultation.
  • Add-ons: a gloss, toner or Olaplex treatment adds to the total.

Ways to save

New clients get buy 4, get 1 free on women's color, starting on the first visit. Balayage also stretches the time between appointments, which lowers what you spend over a year.
