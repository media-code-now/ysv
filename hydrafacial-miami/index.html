Our facials

Treat the concern, respect the skin type

We treat every client by their skin concerns and skin type, choosing the products, technology and techniques that get you to healthy, even, glowing skin.

The signature HydraGlow facial uses wet/dry microdermabrasion to polish the skin, extract blackheads and infuse serums in one treatment.

Questions

Good to know

Which facial should I book first?

Our customized facial is made for first-timers. If you want deeper hydration and extraction, choose the HydraGlow.

Is there downtime?

Most facials have none. Microneedling and RF microneedling can leave skin pink for a day or two; your esthetician will go over aftercare.
